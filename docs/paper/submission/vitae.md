# Author biographies (Vitae)

The Journal of Computational Science asks for a short biography of each author, at most 100
words, in an editable format (Word, not PDF). `scripts/build_jocs_package.py` turns the two
sections below into `jocs_upload/Author_biographies.docx`, counts the words, and refuses to
call the bundle complete while any `[[FILL: ...]]` marker remains.

Only facts already on record are written in. Position, degree and the second author's
research interests are left as markers because they are not recorded anywhere in this
repository; fill them in, then rebuild.
